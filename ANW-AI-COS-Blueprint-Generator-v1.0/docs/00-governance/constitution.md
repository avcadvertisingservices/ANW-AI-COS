# ANW AI-COS Constitution

## Preamble

ANW AI-COS exists to help Acoustic Neuroma Warriors, caregivers, and families access trustworthy education, compassionate support, and practical resources while preserving the principle: **You Are Not Alone.**

## Article I — Mission

Build and operate a survivor-led digital platform that educates, supports, and empowers the Acoustic Neuroma community.

## Article II — Vision

Become the most trusted global survivor-led education and support platform for Acoustic Neuroma patients, survivors, caregivers, and families.

## Article III — Engineering Principles

- Documentation first
- Modular by design
- Automation first
- Security and privacy by default
- Traceable decisions
- Maintainable systems
- Human approval for high-impact outputs

## Article IV — Medical Responsibility

ANW AI-COS provides educational content and community support. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace licensed medical professionals. Medical claims must be evidence-aware, clearly framed, and reviewed when risk is meaningful.

## Article V — AI Ethics

AI assists; humans remain accountable. AI outputs must be transparent, reviewable, and designed to reduce harm, misinformation, bias, and overconfidence.

## Article VI — Community Standards

Communication must remain respectful, compassionate, inclusive, non-exploitative, and sensitive to different treatment paths and recovery experiences.

## Article VII — Documentation Standards

Every major module, workflow, API, database entity, and architecture decision must be documented and version controlled.

## Article VIII — Release Governance

No production release is complete without testing, documentation review, risk review, and explicit approval.

## Article IX — Long-Term Vision

The platform may expand into multilingual education, directories, community services, recovery tools, caregiver resources, applications, and AI-assisted navigation while staying aligned with the mission.
