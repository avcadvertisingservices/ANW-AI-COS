# 15 — Testing Strategy

## Unit Testing
Topic scoring, deduplication, validation, permissions, and state transitions.

## Integration Testing
Supabase, WordPress, Make.com, Google Drive, AI providers, and analytics ingestion.

## End-to-End Testing
Topic discovery through approval, draft publishing, analytics, and repurposing.

## Security Testing
Unauthorized access, RLS, secret exposure, validation, and role separation.

## Content Quality Testing
Factual support, duplication, tone, disclaimers, accessibility, platform fit, and brand compliance.

## User Acceptance Testing
The founder must complete the main workflow with minimal steps and without technical knowledge.
