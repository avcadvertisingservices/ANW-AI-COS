# 04 — Website Information Architecture

## Primary Navigation
Home, Learn, Symptoms, Diagnosis, Treatment, Recovery, Resources, Community, Blog, Videos, About, Contact.

## Core Hubs
Newly Diagnosed; Before Surgery; Watch and Wait; Radiation; Surgery; Recovery; Hearing; Balance; Tinnitus; Facial Nerve; Eye Care; Brain Fog and Fatigue; Mental Health; Caregivers; Hospital Bag; Questions for the Medical Team; Patient Stories; FAQs.

## Required Pages
Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Medical Disclaimer, Cookie Notice, Starter Guide Landing Page, Donation/Support Page, Search.

## Content Types
Educational article, personal story, FAQ, resource guide, checklist, downloadable, video page, community story, affiliate resource, research explainer.

## Publishing Rule
Only high-value, evergreen, frequently requested, or proven social topics should be expanded into permanent website resources.
