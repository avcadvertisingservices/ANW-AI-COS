# 09 — Repurposing Engine

## Strategy
- 20% new content
- 80% repurposed or refreshed winning content

## Winner Levels
- **Gold:** guides, series, lead magnets, cornerstone articles, recurring campaigns.
- **Silver:** refreshed hooks, visuals, formats, and audience angles.
- **Bronze:** archive for seasonal or future reuse.

## Performance Dimensions
Views, reach, watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, meaningful comments, website clicks, followers gained, revenue, evergreen value, and community usefulness.

## Angle Library
Personal story, emotional, educational, myth/fact, caregiver, before surgery, recovery, doctor questions, poll, checklist, mistakes, lessons, product support, faith, short-form hook, long-form guide.
