# 02 — Vision and Mission

## Vision
To become the world's most trusted survivor-led digital education and support platform for acoustic neuroma patients, survivors, caregivers, and families.

## Mission
**You Are Not Alone.**

Provide trusted education, compassionate support, practical resources, and community connection throughout diagnosis, treatment, recovery, and survivorship.

## Content Mission
**Create once. Educate thousands. Inspire forever.**

## Principles
- Mission before technology
- Trust before speed
- Human approval before sensitive publishing
- Permanent knowledge before disposable posting
- Reuse before unnecessary recreation
- Simplicity before complexity
- Privacy before convenience
