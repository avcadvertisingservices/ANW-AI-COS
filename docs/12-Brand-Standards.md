# 12 — Brand Standards

## Core Message
**You Are Not Alone.**

## Tagline
**AWARE. ADVOCATE. INSPIRE.**

## Palette
Dark emerald green, white, soft sage, pale cream, light gray.

## Visual Direction
Calm, premium, compassionate health-education aesthetic. Default social format is 9:16 with a small official ANW shield logo and subtle `acousticneuromawarrior.com` branding.

## Voice
Compassionate, clear, survivor-led, hopeful, honest, medically responsible, never alarmist, never dismissive.

## Content Rule
Personal experience must be labeled as personal experience. Medical education must be supported by trusted references and must not promise outcomes.
