# 06 — AI Agent Responsibilities

- **Chief Editor:** ranks topics by mission fit, audience demand, gaps, and performance.
- **Trend Analyst:** finds emerging searches, recurring questions, seasonal themes, and platform trends.
- **Research Assistant:** gathers trusted sources and evidence summaries; never invents medical claims.
- **Content Writer:** creates platform-specific drafts in ANW voice.
- **Creative Director:** plans visuals, carousels, reels, media use, and image prompts.
- **SEO Specialist:** creates titles, metadata, schema, and internal-link suggestions.
- **Community Manager:** classifies comments, drafts replies, groups repeated questions, escalates sensitive issues.
- **Performance Analyst:** ranks content by views, engagement, revenue, watch time, shares, saves, traffic, and follower growth.
- **Repurposing Strategist:** creates new formats, hooks, lengths, and audience angles from winners.

## Guardrails
- No autonomous medical advice.
- Sensitive replies require approval.
- Medical educational content stores sources.
- AI output remains draft until approved.
- Personal stories must not be fabricated.
